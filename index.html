Monthly rules

  • Maximum 80 counted matches per month.
  • Maximum 5 counted matches against the same opponent per month.
  • At least 40 counted matches for award eligibility.
  • At least 5 different COBEG XI opponents, within the 80-match limit.

Submitting a result does not count it automatically. It must be approved.

CLUB RULES

Rules

How matches count, how the monthly award works, and how BPC competitions are run.

On this pageMonthly leagueMonthly awardSubmitting resultsBPC TrioOther tournamentsFair play
1

Monthly league

  • Every approved and counted friendly match earns points: 3 for a win, 1 for a draw, 0 for a loss.
  • At most 80 counted matches per player each month.
  • At most 5 counted matches between the same two players each month. This includes matches either of you submitted.
  • Matches over these limits are still approved and kept in your history, but they do not count for the table.
  • The table is ordered by points, then goal difference, then wins, then head-to-head points.
2

Monthly award

To be eligible for the monthly award, a player needs, within the same month:

  • at least 40 counted matches,
  • of which at least 25 against COBEG XI players,
  • covering at least 5 different COBEG XI players.

The award ring on the leaderboard and the tracker on your dashboard show your progress.

3

Submitting results

  • Enter the final score exactly as played, with the correct opponent and date.
  • A result counts only after an admin approves it. Pending or rejected results do not affect the table.
  • An admin may reject a result that is wrong, duplicated or cannot be confirmed.
4

BPC Trio

  • Teams. Each team has 3 players in a fixed original lineup: 1st, 2nd and 3rd pick. Only the Management Committee can change it.
  • Fixtures. Each fixture is three 1v1 games: 1st pick v 1st pick, 2nd v 2nd, 3rd v 3rd. The team that wins more games takes 3 points; a draw is 1 point.
  • Home and swap. Every fixture has a home team, and home games are shared out evenly. Only the home captain may switch their 1st and 2nd pick, once per fixture, before the deadline and before boards 1 or 2 are played. The 3rd pick stays, and there is no counter-swap.
  • Walkover. If a player cannot play a round, their captain reports it and an admin confirms. That board goes 3–0 to the opponent. If both players are missing, neither team gets the board.
  • Replacement. A player who leaves the tournament is replaced by the Management Committee in the same slot, before the round starts. Each team has up to 2 replacements.
  • Man of the Match. The winning captain chooses the MOTM from their own players who played, within 24 hours. After that, an admin chooses.
5

Other tournaments

Every tournament lists its own format, deadlines and scoring on its Info tab. Where a tournament's own rules differ from this page, the tournament's rules apply.

See tournaments

6

Fair play

  • Play your own matches on your own account, and report scores honestly.
  • Treat opponents and admins with respect, in games and in the club group.
  • The Management Committee has the final say on disputes and on anything these rules do not cover.
